Hello Jim,

It's a pleasure working with you, and we're excited to begin this journey together.

Below is a detailed overview of our onboarding process to ensure a smooth and structured setup for your campaign:

1. Service Activation
To get started, please sign up for the service via our secure Stripe checkout using the link below. Once on the page, click the "Pay & Join" button to complete your registration:
https://stepup.one/founder-gtm

2. LinkedIn Access Setup
To effectively run the campaign on your behalf, we will require access to your LinkedIn account. Please share the login credentials (email and password) associated with the account. Rest assured, this will be handled with strict confidentiality and used solely for campaign execution.

3. LinkedIn Profile Optimization
If your LinkedIn profile is not already optimized for outreach and conversion, our team will first transform it. This includes refining your positioning, messaging, and overall profile structure to align with your target audience and campaign objectives.

4. Communication Channel Setup
We will create a dedicated WhatsApp group to streamline communication and coordination. This group will include:

- Our CEO, who will oversee the overall campaign strategy
- A Team Leader responsible for managing operations
- A Campaign Specialist who will handle daily execution and outreach

5. CRM Implementation
A dedicated CRM system will be set up to track all campaign activities, monitor engagement, and manage your pipeline efficiently. This ensures full transparency and structured follow-ups throughout the campaign.

6. ICP Sourcing & Messaging Development
Our team will identify and curate your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) based on your target market. We will also develop a tailored messaging sequence designed to engage prospects effectively and drive meaningful conversations.

7. Campaign Review & Launch
Before going live, we will walk you through all campaign materials, including targeting, messaging, and workflow, for your review and approval. Once finalized, we will proceed with the campaign launch.

If you have any questions at any stage, please feel free to reach out. We're here to ensure everything runs seamlessly and delivers strong results.

Looking forward to getting started.
