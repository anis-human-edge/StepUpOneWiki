### **Summary**

The meeting finalized LinkedIn profile narratives and clarified the operational framework for 3 tier sales and engagement engines.  
  
**LinkedIn Profile Narrative Strategy**  
The team resolved to open the profile description with a specific hook regarding small things to ensure story continuity. Metaphors like river references were removed to maintain clarity for potential clients.  
  
**Defining Ideal Customer Profiles**  
Potential clients were segmented into 3 tiers consisting of startups, scaleups, and enterprises to allow for more tailored outreach. Enterprise clients will receive a more specialized account based sales approach.  
  
**Operational Sales Engine Framework**  
Strategies were formalized into independent connection, engagement, and campaign engines to streamline prospect interactions. Engagement protocols were established to ensure value is delivered only after connections meet specific timing criteria.

  
  

### **Decisions**

## **Aligned**

  - **About section content strategy defined** The 'About' section content will remove the river analogy, start with the phrase "By now you must be wondering," and focus exclusively on the contrast between 'big things' and 'small things'.
  - **ICP prioritization hierarchy established** The client acquisition strategy will follow a hierarchical order, prioritizing startups first, followed by scale-ups, and then enterprise accounts.
  - **New prospect testing strategy** The team will test new campaign ideas with new connections first before deploying them to existing contacts to avoid disrupting ongoing relationships.
  - **Three-engine model strategic framework adopted** The team will transition from a single-sequence approach to running three separate, independent engines: Connection, Engagement, and Campaign.
  - **Early prospect engagement protocol established** The team will proceed directly with engagement protocols if a prospect initiates interaction during the connection stage, rather than forcing them to wait.
  - **Network-wide engagement targeting strategy set** Engagement and campaign engines are set to target the entire network of existing connections, rather than limiting outreach to only the most recent connections.
