## **Client onboarding and account outreach structure**

  - Account outreach requires targeting workspaces across 50 accounts, with specific team member assignments.
  - Client onboarding workflows require workspace creation, team addition, and work desk alignment.
  - LinkedIn data zip files and new target lists must be uploaded directly into the studio.
  - Agreements between graduates and clients authorize LinkedIn data uploads and prospecting activities.

  

## **Studio onboarding and leaderboard tracking**

  - Team members must log into the studio using personal credentials for accurate leaderboard tracking.
  - Sharing accounts across the platform obscures individual performance metrics and leaderboard standings.
  - Comment points accumulate automatically in the studio without explicitly adding post links.

  

## **Professional commenting framework and skill taxonomy**

  - The commenting curriculum defines 24 beginner skills, intermediate tiers, and 8 expert categories.
  - Professional commenting requires mastering specific stylistic terms rather than relying on intuitive feedback.
  - Experienced commenters recognize specific stylistic flaws like parrot responses and question dumps instantly.

  

## **Verifiable career proof and professional dashboards**

  - Traditional certificates offer limited career value for interviews compared to verifiable public work history.
  - Professional dashboards store objective career metrics including active years, client counts, and comment volumes.
  - Verifiable proof systems link historical comments directly to live social media URLs to prevent fabrication.

  

## **Dashboard concept and inspiration**

  - Mohamed Anis drew inspiration from Khan Academy and Bill Gates to build comprehensive professional dashboards.
  - Bill Gates predicted that comprehensive learning dashboards would eventually surpass Harvard PhD credentials in value.
  - Harvard and Khan Academy recently launched a joint course incorporating tracking dashboards costing $10,000 to $15,000.
  - Individual dashboards automatically track daily tasks, consistency, skills, and evidence libraries over a lifetime.

  

## **Learning, practicing, and performing framework**

  - The structured framework requires members to progress through distinct learning, practicing, and performing stages.
  - Learning and practicing occur within designated studio lessons while performance happens directly through client work.
  - Completed lessons and scores are permanently recorded against individual profiles to build verifiable professional track records.

  

## **Historical work documentation challenges**

  - Team members noted a major documentation gap for professional work completed over the past 7 years.
