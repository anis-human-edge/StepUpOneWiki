- They realized fundraising was not a compounding or highly profitable model — 97% of clients did not raise capital, engagements only lasted 3–6 months.
- They shifted focus to capture the 60–70% of raised capital founders spend on go-to-market strategies.
- They further expanded by targeting investors as "Plan B" support for portfolio companies, and more recently supporting fractional CXOs.

## Core Offerings and AI Integration

- Multi-channel client engagement — LinkedIn, Twitter, email — AI-driven, zero automation.
- "AI Deploy Co" branch as official Anthropic partner helping organizations shift AI from peripheral to core operating systems.
- Goal: turn traditional finance/accounting/payroll into AI-native setups for instant projections.

## The Vision for an Autonomous Company

- Building a 100% AI-native company to replace Web 2.0 systems (CRMs, Notion, email).

- Ideal target: founder-led companies with $5–100M revenue lacking a full-time CFO.
- Highly asynchronous service model; tiered pricing (frontier models to open-source/on-premise).
- Lukasz narrowing to cash flow management; targeting Germany and Poland markets.
