Discussion regarding the Account Based Sales and Marketing strategy finalized a 3 month engagement cycle for high value accounts.  
  
**Refining Account Based Strategy**  
The team transitioned away from volume-based sales tactics to focus on high-value, multi-threaded relationships. This approach prioritizes sustainable engagement for large enterprise clients rather than transactional outreach.

Best practices include withdrawing connection requests after 30 days and limiting pending invites to maintain account health. These protocols ensure long-term stability and prevent platform penalties during complex outreach campaigns.
