Platform onboarding procedures and relationship scoring strategies via targeted list management methods

  

## **Platform Onboarding and Workspace Management**

  - Create a dedicated client workspace using the client name as the identifier.
  - Establish a client desk and assign team leaders and members to manage accounts.
  - Distinguish between contact books from devices and target lists for new relationships.
  - Execute client agreements manually within the workspace during the current phase.

  

## **ABSM Platform Purpose and Relationship Categories**

  - Focus the ABSM platform entirely on building new relationships and converting existing ones.
  - Group contacts by company for sales contexts and by region for investor fundraising.

  

## **Quantitative Relationship Scoring Framework**

  - Measure relationship strength using a numerical scoring system based on interaction frequency.
  - Award baseline points for platform connections and public social media post interactions.
  - Assign higher weight to client-initiated conversations compared to outbound messages.
  - Deduct points for purely transactional and repetitive administrative inquiries like monthly receipt requests.

  

## **Human Edge relationship capital platform concept**

  - Human Edge operates as a system to measure and quantify relationship capital.
  - Relationship capital scores function similarly to financial bank account balances.
  - The platform evaluates relationship strength on a scale from initial contact to long-term engagement.

  

## **Teaching methodology and team participation**

  - Mohamed Anis emphasized active team participation to ensure comprehension of platform fundamentals.
  - Team members proposed streamlining training sessions and incorporating daily review mechanisms.
  - Mohamed Anis noted ongoing improvements to automated learning and feedback systems.

  

## **Client workspace setup and team assignment**

  - Client onboarding requires creating a dedicated workspace and assigning internal team members.
  - Users must establish formal agreements with client stakeholders before managing their data.
  - Client organizational hierarchies are populated by adding key stakeholders and mapping them to team members.

  

## **Importing existing and new relationships**

  - Users import historical data by uploading exported LinkedIn zip archives into the workspace.
  - The platform filters out irrelevant entries from imported lists before designating target audiences.
  - Teams identify missing stakeholders using Sales Navigator to expand outreach and build new relationships.

  

## **Relationship management and contact imports**

  - Mohamed Anis outlined the 5-step process separating existing connections from new target lists.
  - The system automatically reconciles uploaded contact lists with existing database records to identify new relationship opportunities.
  - Users can import large stakeholder lists based on ICP criteria or manually handpick specific profiles.

  

## **Outcome creation and strategic pursuit**

  - Mohamed Anis explained that outcomes are created proactively from signals or reactively from RFPs.
  - Teams can build targeted stakeholder groups of up to 20 people to drive specific outcomes.

  

## **Human Edge platform extension features**

  - Mohamed Anis previewed extension capabilities designed to capture web activity and transport data into outcomes.
  - The platform extension integrates external browsing workflows with CRM tracking to streamline opportunity management.
