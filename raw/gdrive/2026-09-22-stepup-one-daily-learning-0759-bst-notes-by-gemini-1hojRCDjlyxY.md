LinkedIn commenting strategies debated with engagement metrics and relationship building priorities.

  

## **LinkedIn commenting objectives and relationship strategy**

  - Farah Ibrahim distinguished between viral commenting for reach and APSM commenting focused on building direct relationships.
  - Commenting on posts published hours or days prior prevents appearing like a stalker on older content.
  - Personal profiles rather than company pages are targeted to effectively establish individual professional relationships.

  

## **Analysis of AI market dependence and revenue metrics**

  - Farah Ibrahim noted that $24 billion of $34 billion in Microsoft AI revenue originated from OpenAI.
  - Microsoft spent $261 billion over 4 years attempting to reduce heavy dependency on a single primary client.

  

## **Engagement metrics and feedback evaluation**

  - Direct replies indicate higher relationship strength than simple likes, according to Mohamed Anis and Farah Ibrahim.
  - Abdulkadir Mhina noted that constructive professional disagreements provide engagement angles while severe negative reactions require comment removal.

  

## **Commenting objectives and strategies**

  - Mohamed Anis identified 4 commenting objectives: relationship building, reach, traffic, and thought leadership.
  - Farah Ibrahim clarified that reach measures impressions while traffic captures website clicks through links.
  - Mohamed Anis noted that adding links to comments usually reduces virality because users perceive it as selling.
  - Thought leadership comments focus on generating massive conversations and professional visibility.

  

## **AI skill assessment and training**

  - Mohamed Anis proposed using AI to test and score commenting talent through 5 choices.
  - Mohamed Anis advised abandoning commenting if users lack natural aptitude, comparing it to unproductive training.
  - Mohamed Anis explained that AI provides immediate feedback explaining why comment choices are correct or incorrect.
  - The system tracks user skill levels continuously to measure progress over time.

  

## **Performance metrics and skill score evaluation**

  - URL clicks take priority over impressions when evaluating performance effectiveness.
  - An impression count of 10,000 paired with zero URL clicks results in a skill score failure of 5%.
  - Mohamed Anis stated that commenting on a comment establishes the baseline for relationship building.

  

## **Company research and social engagement strategy**

  - Participants discussed analyzing assigned companies thoroughly to determine viability for long-term engagement.
  - Sarah Aziz and Kadafi Hamza were identified for initiating social account preparation and following targets.
