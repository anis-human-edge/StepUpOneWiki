## **LinkedIn Engagement and Commenting Strategy**

  - Farah implemented a workflow to identify and comment on relevant LinkedIn posts to establish a professional presence.
  - Mohamed Anis cautioned that generic or average comments are often ignored or viewed negatively by high-level executives.
  - The team discussed adopting a "heavyweight boxer" strategy, prioritizing sharp, high-value insights over comment volume to influence target decision-makers.

  

## **B2B Business Models and IT Services**

  - Mohamed Anis clarified that B2B engagements require formal business-to-business contracts, contrasting this with B2C individual subscription models.
  - IT service firms like Infosys generate revenue primarily through labor-intensive services, scaling through gradual, long-term project expansion.
  - These firms maintain high client retention rates by embedding a "client-first" philosophy into their organizational culture.

  

## **ABSM Strategic Framework**

  - Defined the 3 core ABSM pillars: Break-in ($1M), Expand ($10M), and Protect ($100M).
  - Prioritized account importance, ranking Protect as 100 times more critical than Break-in, and Expand as 10 times more critical.
  - Highlighted the risk of losing top accounts, noting that small firms face business closure, while large corporations risk multi-billion dollar market cap devaluation.
  - Advised aiming for high-value targets, noting that ambitious goal-setting yields stronger positioning even if only intermediate objectives are met.

  

## **Client Engagement Methodology**

  - Advised against selling quality through marketing claims, favoring a 'show, don't tell' approach where value is demonstrated through direct experience.
  - Instructed the team to prove capability by sharing visual evidence of previous work, such as relationship maps and past engagement histories.
  - Established a quality requirement that all content, including comments and posts, must be 'showcase worthy' to maintain credibility.

  

## **Platform Strategy**

  - Classified LinkedIn as essential for master data, but ineffective as a platform for active, current discussion.
  - Identified Twitter as the primary channel for immediate, viral industry insights compared to the slower pace of LinkedIn.
