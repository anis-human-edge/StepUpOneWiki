## **Account-Based Sales Marketing (ABSM) methodology**

  - ABSM is defined as the strategic process of hunting accounts rather than individual contacts.
  - For enterprise-scale accounts, the methodology requires decomposing the organization into manageable units rather than treating the company as a singular target.
  - The follow the money principle serves as the primary framework for identifying the most commercially significant units within an organization.
  - Successful ABSM requires mapping internal organizational structures to locate the specific stakeholders who hold decision-making authority.

  

## **Organizational structure analysis (Cognizant case study)**

  - Mohamed clarified that Cognizant's US division is primarily organized by industry rather than by geography.
  - Large corporate hierarchies typically follow a progression from region to industry to specific accounts.
  - Stakeholder mapping must account for centralized decision-making; regional projects may be determined by headquarters in a different country rather than local management.
  - Segmenting massive organizations by industry creates smaller, actionable groups of approximately 100 leaders, improving outreach efficiency.

  

## **Strategic Market Penetration Analysis**

  - Mohamed highlighted that Cognizant generates $15 billion in total revenue, with $2-3 billion derived from Europe.
  - Regional barriers and language differences in Europe necessitate an organizational division by country, such as the UK, Germany, and France.
  - Account-level penetration is identified as the fundamental strategy, as all sales activity must eventually focus on specific accounts.
  - Top-level leadership roles, including CEOs, CFOs, and CMOs, exist outside of standard hierarchical structures and require separate connection strategies.
