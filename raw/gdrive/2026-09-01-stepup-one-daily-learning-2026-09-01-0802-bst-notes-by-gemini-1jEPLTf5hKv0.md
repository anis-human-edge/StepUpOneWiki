## **Enterprise Account Campaign Strategy**

  - Ahmed identified 99 enterprise accounts within the IT service and consulting sector with growth rates exceeding 10%.
  - Proposed utilizing a tiered classification where ABSM serves as the primary ICP, with team names assigned as sub-ICPs.
  - Team members will manage dedicated account lists to ensure focused, top-down outreach to senior decision-makers.
  - The group planned to evaluate connection success and strategy efficacy after a 1-month trial period.

  

## **Revenue-Focused Strategic Mindset**

  - Mohamed Anis challenged the team to transition from task-based workflows to outcome-oriented, winning strategies.
  - Effective account penetration requires identifying specific client pain points and presenting unique, relevant solutions.
  - Revenue remains the ultimate business objective, with relationship building acting as the tactical pathway to success.
  - Participants analyzed the concept of urgency, debating methods to accelerate account conversion beyond standard, long-term cycles.

  

## **Strategic ABSM Framework**

  - The primary objective of the ABSM strategy is to deliver revenue growth for clients, as this is the most critical pain point.
  - Intent-led selling utilizes specific context (e.g., funding, promotions) to initiate direct, professional outreach.
  - Mixing relationship-led outreach and intent-led pitching on the same account simultaneously risks appearing uncoordinated and unprofessional.

  

## **Account Targeting and Filtering Criteria**

  - Established strict qualification criteria for target accounts: minimum 1,000 employees and US-based headquarters.
  - Identified high-density operations in India as a strategic flaw for certain sectors due to cost and productivity comparisons.
  - Proposed pharmaceutical manufacturing as a priority industry for new lead sourcing to improve targeting focus.

  

## **Operational Engagement Workflow**

  - Established a top-down engagement model: prioritize connecting with CEOs first, followed sequentially by CXOs, VPs, and Directors.
  - Recommended segregating relationship-led and intent-led campaigns by account to maintain a clear, unified point of contact.

  

## **ABSM Framework and Methodology**

  - Mohamed Anis proposed integrating signals, intent, and relationship-building into a single, cohesive account-based strategy.
  - The strategy targets long-term enterprise deals with 3 to 6 month cycles by engaging multiple departments.
  - Team members must monitor target accounts daily to identify engagement opportunities in real-time.
  - Distinguished between signals like minor updates and intent such as hiring or job changes.
  - Proposed allocating direct pitches for high-intent moments, while using general connections for the remaining 95% of contacts.

  

## **Industry Pilot Implementation**

  - Ahmed Farhan will lead the pharmaceutical industry pilot, focusing on high-intent monitoring and pitching.
  - Designated IT services as a secondary dataset to test broad team outreach effectiveness.
  - Future scaling will depend on response metrics observed across these pilot industries.
  - Mohamed Anis will determine if separate landing pages are necessary for distinct industry verticals.

  

## **Professional Branding Standards**

  - Mohamed Anis identified current LinkedIn profiles as inadequate for effective engagement with senior stakeholders.
  - Standardized professional profiles are required to maintain credibility when reaching out to C-suite executives and VPs.
