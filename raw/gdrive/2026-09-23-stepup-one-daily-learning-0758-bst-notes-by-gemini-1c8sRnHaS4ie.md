  - Discussed a proposal to divide the team into separate groups for brief reach-outs and ads.

  - Established a 3-phase structure comprising training, practice, and performance phases.
  - Designed a 1 to 5 scoring system measuring initial choices from AI options.
  - Targeted 1000 comments over 100 days to assess commenting capability and award performance badges.
  - Determined that individuals scoring 0% or low talent metrics should transition to alternative tasks.

  - Samatar utilized Sales Navigator to identify posting accounts and filter relevant business development roles.
  - Recommended following company accounts, enabling notifications, and restricting comments to 1 line for safety.

  

## **LinkedIn Connection and Engagement Strategy**

  - Mohamed Anis proposed prioritizing senior professionals who actively post during the first 3 months.
  - Cold connections without a specific context or reason are prohibited.
  - Ninety percent of senior professionals in IT services rarely publish posts, requiring alternative engagement triggers.
  - Connecting and commenting within 24 hours of a relevant post ensures optimal engagement.

  

## **News-Based Outreach Triggers and Account Scaling**

  - Leveraging major company announcements serves as an immediate trigger to congratulate CEOs.
  - Reacting to major news milestones within hours guarantees high response rates from senior executives.
  - Scaling outreach operations from 1 account to 5 accounts is planned after mastering current workflows.

  

## **Strategic Thinking and AI Integration**

  - Mohamed Anis emphasized prioritizing deep strategic thinking and account research over high-volume tactical execution.
  - AI handles routine execution tasks while team members focus on strategic evaluation.
