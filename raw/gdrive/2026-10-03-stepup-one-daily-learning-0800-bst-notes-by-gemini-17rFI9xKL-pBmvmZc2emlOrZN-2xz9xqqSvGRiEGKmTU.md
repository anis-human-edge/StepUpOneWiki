## **ABSM campaign outreach and core services**

  - Outreach prioritization targets client partners first, followed by BDRs and sales roles.
  - The ABSM campaign focuses on 3 core services: breaking in, expanding, and protecting.
  - Breaking in involves winning new client accounts, while expanding targets additional departments.
  - Protecting maintains existing client revenue streams to prevent loss to competing firms.

  

## **Conceptual learning and principles**

  - Mohamed Anis noted that team members struggle with basic conceptual questions during execution.
  - Mohamed Anis shared a personal 8-year cooking experience to illustrate true comprehension development.
  - Achieving conceptual grip requires understanding fundamental components and big-picture perspectives rather than memorizing.

  

## **Evolution from GTM to ABSM Strategies**

  - Mohamed Anis noted that previous 7-year GTM campaigns relied on large-scale prospect outreach.
  - ABSM shifts the core focus from broad volume outreach to deep account penetration and relationship building.
  - The fundamental principle of ABSM centers on establishing trusted relationships before initiating commercial transactions.

  

## **Relationship-Driven Revenue and Value Creation**

  - Mohamed Anis highlighted that business revenue originates directly from nurturing professional relationships rather than transactional outreach.
  - Building high-value connections enables professionals to convert network engagement into substantial financial returns.
  - Personal relationships provide foundational value comparable to household management and parenting contributions.

  

## **Combining Scale with Targeted Relationship Building**

  - Explored combining large-scale reach-out methods with targeted relationship building to maximize business impact.
  - Mohamed Anis noted that executing scale and relationship building simultaneously remains extremely difficult due to time constraints.
  - Farhaan Abdi hassan suggested expanding target account volume to 10 accounts simultaneously to scale relationship efforts.

  

## **CEO relationship network and client trust**

  - Mohamed Anis identified 124 tier 1 CEO relationships across large companies exceeding 10,000 employees.
  - Clients would hesitate to entrust top-tier executive networks to team members without proven reliability.

  

## **AI-powered relationship building and skill tracking framework**

  - Ahmed Farhan demonstrated a 5-step workflow combining AI recommendations with human judgment for commenting and posting.
  - The ABSM framework categorizes team members into beginners, skilled practitioners, and top performers based on experience.
  - Skill levels in go-to-market and capital raising are measured through tests and evidence.
  - Personal and client-facing views are live on the website to prove competence to enterprise clients.
