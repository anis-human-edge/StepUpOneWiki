## **Account Based Sales and Marketing (ABSM) Framework**

  - Farah explained that large company structures, such as Cognizant, operate with a CEO at the top, followed by EVP or Presidents per geography, then industry verticals.
  - Mohamed noted that account hierarchies vary, with 1 to 4 levels separating the client partner from the CEO depending on the account scale.
  - Mohamed instructed teams to prioritize top-to-bottom connectivity by reaching out to all CXOs and VPs at the account level.

  

## **Clarification of Sales Terminology**

  - Mohamed differentiated account size (total annual revenue generated) from deal size (the value of a specific, active transaction).
  - Mohamed clarified that outbound sales involve direct outreach by the sales team, whereas inbound refers to marketing-driven leads.
  - Farah acknowledged that RFPs are results of marketing efforts rather than traditional inbound sales leads.

  

## **Team Discipline and Operational Leadership**

  - Mohamed emphasized that daily 10:00 to 11:00 sessions are mandatory to demonstrate team discipline and ABSM process understanding.
  - Mohamed warned that leaders who fail to enforce team discipline risk long-term failure despite potential short-term financial gains.
  - Farah confirmed that attendance is enforced within the team to ensure consistent application of ABSM strategies.

  

## **Platform Development and Strategy Execution**

  - Mohamed reported that the website is near completion and ready for publication.
  - Mohamed proposed initiating connection strategies in parallel with training to accelerate progress rather than waiting to finish all theoretical learning.

  

## **Warm Introduction Strategy for Account Acquisition**

  - Mohamed emphasized a top-down strategy, prioritizing connections with CEOs to facilitate access to the broader account.
  - The team aligned on the warm introduction strategy as the primary method for high-level outreach.
  - Mohamed advised reserving personal name-dropping for approximately 20 specific executives to maintain credibility and prevent over-saturation.
  - Farah suggested conducting specific research on target executives to personalize outreach and improve engagement quality.
  - Farah flagged potential data inaccuracies in Sales Navigator that may be inflating the perceived count of target CXOs.

  

## **Website and Messaging Strategy**

  - Mohamed presented a revised website layout designed for clarity, avoiding sophisticated jargon to ensure immediate understanding.
  - The site will feature scroll-based animations detailing 3 core commercial priorities: breaking into, expanding, and protecting accounts.
  - The messaging framework incorporates 5 questions to articulate client problems and positions ABSM as the solution.
  - Farah and the team validated the initial layout, noting the content provides a solid, clear foundation for further improvements.
