### **Summary**

The team refined branding strategies and professional titles while maintaining existing campaign operations for improved corporate engagement.  
  
**Brand Messaging and Strategy**  
The team debated content clarity versus curiosity for social profiles, ultimately prioritizing messaging that addresses specific client pain points. They consolidated their methodology into a structured approach focused on acquiring, protecting, and growing accounts.  
  
**Corporate Identity and Titles**  
Participants concluded that internal titles like team leader lack professional credibility with high-level executives. They decided to adopt standard titles such as Vice President of Account-Based Growth to better align with target audiences.  
  
**Campaign Integration and Focus**  
The team reached a decision to maintain successful legacy fundraising and executive outreach campaigns alongside new Account-Based Sales and Marketing efforts. They agreed to restrict the new strategy exclusively to new account acquisitions to avoid high-risk interference.
