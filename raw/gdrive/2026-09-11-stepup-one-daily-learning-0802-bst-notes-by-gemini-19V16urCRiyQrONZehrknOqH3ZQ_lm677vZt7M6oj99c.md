## **ABSM Core Concepts and Strategy**

  - The core of ABSM strategy is hunting an account rather than targeting individuals, sectors, or industries.
  - The differentiating factor in ABSM is the "account" focus, likened to the role breadcrumbs play in fried chicken.
  - Large accounts require "chunking" to isolate specific buying centers rather than attempting mass engagement.
  - A buying center is defined as the group within an account responsible for a specific purchasing decision.
  - Buying centers must be filtered by relevance and decision-making authority to ensure resources are focused on active prospects.

  

## **Account Mapping and Relationship Building**

  - Effective account strategies involve analyzing organizational structures to identify specific, addressable buying centers.
  - Successful mapping requires identifying primary decision-makers, such as a CIO, and key influencers within the account.
  - Relationship management relies on tracking interaction strength and maintaining notes on individual contacts within identified tribes.

  - Mohamed Anis emphasized the requirement for punctuality, active participation, and the courage to admit when concepts are not understood.
