## **LinkedIn data export and synchronization limitations**

  - LinkedIn exports omit essential connection or conversation CSV files due to platform-side data generation errors.
  - Automated syncing between LinkedIn profiles and internal platforms remains unsupported without manual archive imports.

  

## **Relationship recommendation filtering and country prioritization**

  - Relationship recommendation algorithms incorrectly prioritize high-value financial metrics over geographic relevance.
  - Location filters were introduced to refine suggestions and exclude irrelevant international profiles from relationship lists.

  

## **Historical software adoption challenges and CRM flexibility**

  - Sales professionals historically avoided traditional CRMs due to rigid interfaces and cumbersome manual data entry.
  - Users preferred spreadsheets because adaptable structures accommodated custom charting and rapid data manipulation.

  

## **Traditional CRM architecture and terminology**

  - Discussed traditional CRM architecture elements including contacts, leads, opportunities, deals, and pipelines.
  - Farhaan Abdi hassan proposed incorporating familiar CRM language to improve user familiarity.
  - Explored balancing standard CRM terminology with outcome-driven user experiences for sales professionals.

  - Highlighted an import volume of 779900 WhatsApp messages across 6300 people.
  - Explored utilizing real-time company news alerts and breach reports to identify sales opportunities.

  

## **Data privacy and filtering controls**

  - Nuuh Iftin raised concerns regarding data privacy and administrator access over imported user communications.
  - Mohamed Anis explained that message visibility remains strictly individual to ensure data privacy despite administrative rights.
  - Discussed filtering out specific companies, competitors, and irrelevant contacts from daily relationship lists.

  - Mohamed Anis proposed organizing accounts into specific buying centers, such as European CTOs for CrowdStrike.
  - Discussed defining precise, reachable outcomes and target values rather than overly broad revenue goals.
