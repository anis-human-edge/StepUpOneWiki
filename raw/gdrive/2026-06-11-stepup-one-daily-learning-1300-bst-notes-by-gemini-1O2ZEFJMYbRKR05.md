- **Influencer Monitoring Strategy**: Farhaan Abdi hassan proposed a strategy of identifying and following top industry influencers, turning on notifications for their activity, and commenting as soon as they publish content to capture relevant traffic. - **Defining the Two-Part Rule for Trending Content**: Mohamed Anis outlined a fundamental rule for identifying trending content: first, track trending companies—citing examples such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepMind, Scale AI, and Grok AI—and second, track trending people or influencers. Mohamed Anis and Farhaan Abdi hassan worked through technical challenges in LinkedIn's search filters, noting that searching for too many items simultaneously can cause errors, requiring them to refine the search lists or add entities one by one to ensure the functionality works correctly (00:37:57) (00:43:37).
- **Content Creation and Visual Strategy**: Addressing potential concerns from Samatar Abdirahman regarding content creation, Farhaan Abdi hassan noted that posts do not always require images to be effective.
