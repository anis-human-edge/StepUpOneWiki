  - Mohamed sharmuge Mohamed expressed concern over declining meeting volume, identifying only 3 upcoming sessions scheduled for September 7 and 8.
  - Management advised the team to refocus efforts on existing CRM gold-tier opportunities rather than prioritizing unproven new initiatives.

  

## **AI Research Methodology**

  - Mohamed Anis challenged the quality of recent AI research on Fizer, noting that the AI provided inaccurate generalizations about sales team behavior.
  - Mohamed Anis advised the team to rigorously challenge AI outputs and avoid accepting logical inconsistencies or platform-generated assumptions as fact.
  - Mohamed Anis recommended utilizing voice interaction with AI to enable conversational reasoning and increase the volume of investigation.

  

## **ABSM Account Strategy and Decision Hierarchies**

  - Mohamed Anis illustrated account-based sales strategy by mapping organizational structures, emphasizing the need to target specific departments rather than broad audiences.
  - Building relationships with CXOs and final approvers is critical, as they serve as the ultimate decision-making authority for high-value deals.
  - Effective deal closing requires identifying and influencing the entire buying committee, which includes influencers and departmental heads beyond the primary champion.

  

## **Deal mapping and relationship strategy**

  - Mohamed Anis proposed a hierarchical relationship mapping framework to secure large deals, prioritizing top-tier decision makers to cascade influence.
  - The strategy involves categorizing contacts by role—including decision makers, evaluators, and procurement—tailored specifically to the territory.

  

## **Pfizer target account evaluation**

  - Mohamed Anis highlighted challenges in identifying target departments within Pfizer, noting a lack of the clarity seen in the Standard Chartered account.
  - Participants determined that unlike technology sales, Pfizer's medical-focused model requires prioritizing medical and pharmacy stakeholders over IT departments.
