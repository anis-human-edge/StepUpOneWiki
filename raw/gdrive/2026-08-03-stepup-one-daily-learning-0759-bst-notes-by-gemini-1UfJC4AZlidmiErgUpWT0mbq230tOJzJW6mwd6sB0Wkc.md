### **Summary**

The team adopted Account Based Sales and Marketing methodologies to improve retention while refining long-term targeting strategies.  
  
**Refined Client Engagement Strategy**  
The team shifted focus from transactional fundraising services to long-term Account Based Sales and Marketing engagements. Prioritizing well-funded organizations ensures greater financial stability for future campaigns.  
  
**Sales Methodology Implementation**  
A 3-engine framework was established for connection, engagement, and pitching processes. These components require separate performance metrics to effectively manage the sales funnel.  
  
**Scaling Operational Capabilities**  
Current customer relationship management tools only partially support the required methodology. Refining processes internally provides a foundation before expanding to target markets with 10 million to 100 million in revenue.
