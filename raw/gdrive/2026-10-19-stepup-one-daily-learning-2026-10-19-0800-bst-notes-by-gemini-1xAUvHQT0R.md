  - Farah proposed combining conceptual understanding with deliberate practice to progress toward level 3 competence.

  

## **Social media digital footprint and comment quality**

  - Mohamed Anis flagged that poor social media comments damage professional credibility with potential clients.
  - Farah noted that public comments represent clients, making comment quality more critical than quantity.
  - Nuuh Iftin suggested responding professionally and accepting feedback constructively rather than deleting critiques.

  

## **Comment evaluation framework and professional standards**

  - Mohamed Anis introduced a training exercise evaluating 5 comment options to teach professional standards.
  - The team identified parroting as an unacceptable flaw where comments merely repeat author statements.
  - Mohamed Abdullahi Farah highlighted that excessive praising comments lack substantive value and require rejection.
  - Mohamed Anis flagged indecisive phrasing using speculative words as an unacceptable comment flaw.

  

## **Commenting studio tool features and rollout**

  - Mohamed Anis confirmed the commenting studio is officially ready for team use despite ongoing speed improvements.
  - The tool structures practice into beginner, intermediate, and expert tiers to progressively build commenting proficiency.
  - Beginners evaluate 5 options to identify the correct comment and learn why the others fail.
  - The system automatically tracks user streaks, scores, and earned badges to gamify the learning process.

  

## **Professional standards and evaluation criteria for comments**

  - Mohamed Anis emphasized relying on objective reasoning rather than gut feelings to evaluate comment quality.
  - The platform categorizes comment flaws into failure types including wrong voice, off point, and self pitch.
  - High-quality comments add a unique perspective, reinforce author points, or offer relevant counter-examples using natural tones.
  - Mohamed Anis advised team members to maintain professional online profiles by deleting comments written in unnatural tones.
