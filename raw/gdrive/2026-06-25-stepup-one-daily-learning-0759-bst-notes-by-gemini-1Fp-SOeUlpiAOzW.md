### Summary

The team reviewed service models and operational scaling risks while addressing internal communication and leadership development challenges.  
  
**Service and Performance Review**  
The team clarified that monthly subscription models are necessary to cover platform costs. Leaders emphasized transitioning to percentage-based performance analysis rather than viewing absolute volume to improve metrics.  
  
**Operational Scaling Concerns**  
The group identified that scaling client acquisition without clear leadership structures risks service quality. A decision was made to prioritize client relationship management over aggressive new sales growth.  
  
**Leadership and Communication Strategy**  
Team members debated the necessity of commitment versus skill for leadership roles and resolved internal misunderstandings regarding client management protocols. The team committed to personal follow-up strategies for prospective clients.
