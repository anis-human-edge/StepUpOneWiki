Engagement strategy discussion covering LinkedIn tactics and Twitter focus.

  

## **LinkedIn Engagement Strategy**

  - The team discussed balancing direct connection requests with soft engagement to increase visibility with target accounts.
  - Farah suggested interacting with comments on target accounts to build presence before initiating formal connection requests.
  - Mohammed emphasized the necessity of human-generated comments, warning against the reputation risks of AI-produced responses.
  - The team established a policy to proofread comments twice before posting to ensure authenticity.

  

## **Value-Based Outreach Approach**

  - The outreach strategy shifts toward sharing high-credibility industry reports to provide value, rather than generic messaging.
  - Mohammed advised leveraging existing top-tier industry insights (e.g., McKinsey reports) over creating original, large-scale content like podcasts.

  

## **Growth Metrics and Executive Compensation**

  - Mohammed contextualized company performance by explaining industry average growth rates, typically around 4%.
  - The team examined the correlation between exceeding industry growth rates (e.g., 3x performance) and exponential increases in executive compensation.
  - Mohammed illustrated potential significant salary increases for CEOs moving from average growth to high-performance growth.

  

## **ABSM Positioning and Value Proposition**

  - The Account-Based Sales Management (ABSM) strategy involves positioning services to help account managers exceed growth targets.
  - Mohammed characterized target account managers as ambitious and result-oriented, requiring direct, high-value communication.
  - The goal is to frame engagements as direct accelerators for professional compensation, treating every meeting as a revenue-generating opportunity.

  

## **Outbound Messaging Strategy**

  - Mohamed Anis proposed moving away from generic, high-promise outbound claims which recipients perceive as untrustworthy.
  - The team will adopt a strategy focused on achievable, concrete metrics, such as growing 3 times faster than the 3-year average.
  - Outreach must be personalized for each profile, directly acknowledging the recipient's specific role and pain points.
  - The proposed strategy involves a direct, cutthroat approach for outbound messaging, contrasted with supportive inbound engagement.

  

## **Platform Strategy**

  - Mohamed Anis identified LinkedIn as having declining value due to excessive bot activity and low-quality interactions.
  - The group will pivot networking focus toward Twitter to engage with more substantive professional content.
  - Mohamed Anis directed the team to cease sending LinkedIn connection requests, focusing instead on following and commenting.

  

## **Operational Administration**

  - Mohamed Anis highlighted the lack of automated tools to extract employee lists from target companies.
