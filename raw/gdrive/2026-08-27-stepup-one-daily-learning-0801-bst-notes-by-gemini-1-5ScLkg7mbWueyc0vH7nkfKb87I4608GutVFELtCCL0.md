- Emphasized that success is not determined by the pitch alone but by the champion's ability to defend the cause using pre-provided data, value propositions, and key talking points.
- Instructed the team to avoid over-complicating straightforward concepts, urging for direct internalization rather than excessive, PhD-level analysis of simple truths.

- Mandated that future interactions must prioritize thoughtful, reflection-based commenting rather than passive, generic output.

- The team identified a lack of actionable solutions in current messaging regarding how to influence decisions made in rooms where the salesperson is not present.
- Mohamed Anis updated the messaging sequence to emphasize relationship building as the primary driver for influencing deals in absentia.
