## **Account hunting and prospecting strategy**

  - Ahmed Farhan compiled over 1,000 breaking-in accounts and general CXO lists for target prospecting.
  - The team engages prospects by liking and commenting on posts rather than sending direct connection requests.
  - Enterprise accounts like Cognizant and Infosys present significant deal opportunities alongside extended sales cycle risks.
  - Diversifying prospecting efforts across multiple team members serves as an operational insurance policy for pipelines.

  

## **LinkedIn content and engagement quality**

  - Senior executives from target accounts viewed Ahmed Farhan's profile shortly after public commenting activity.
  - Mohamed Anis emphasized that true content virality relies on second- and third-degree network engagement.
  - The team explored focusing content on specific niches and optimal posting times to maximize visibility.

  

## **Enterprise sales and value proposition challenges**

  - The team discussed the difficulty of winning enterprise deals when prospective clients possess advanced internal expertise.
  - Mohamed Anis highlighted that founders buy based on knowledge gaps, unlike experienced enterprise clients.

  

## **The proof trap in professional hiring**

  - Professionals face a proof trap requiring prior work experience to secure employment.
  - Global technology companies struggle to source local talent lacking brand-name enterprise experience.
  - The team analyzed difficulties in winning ABSM clients without established proof or credentials.

  

## **Infosys recruitment and training framework**

  - Infosys recruits 10,000 graduates annually through strict academic cutoffs, common sense exams, and rigorous interviews.
  - The corporation spends $100 million on hiring and $200 million on a 6-month corporate university training program.
  - Talent multiplied by effort generates skill, while skill multiplied by effort drives professional achievement.

  

## **Comparative analysis of earnings and career growth**

  - Delivery drivers earn 40,000 to 80,000 shillings monthly, initially outpacing entry-level software engineers earning 20,000 shillings.
  - Software engineer earnings grow exponentially to 2 million shillings monthly after 10 years, unlike stagnant delivery wages.
