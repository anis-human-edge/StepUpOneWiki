  - The ABSM methodology comprises 20 practical execution steps mirrored from internal operations to client engagements.

  

## **Deal Closure Principles and Sales Analogy**

  - Major business deals are decided in absentia, necessitating trusted internal champions to advocate on behalf of vendors.
  - Selling ABSM parallels property sales, requiring proactive education on product quality, surrounding environment, and pricing justification.
  - Securing a single influential champion inside the target decision-making room is sufficient to drive agreement.

  

## **Core ABSM Execution Steps**

  - The framework progresses through choosing accounts, identifying buying centers, building familiarity, arranging meetings, and sustaining relationships.
  - Buying centers are classified by revenue thresholds exceeding $20 million across specific accounts, geographies, or industry verticals.

  

## **ABSM methodology and account strategy**

  - The core offering centers on ABSM to solve the problem of winning new accounts.
  - Current account strategies focus exclusively on breaking in, leaving protection and expansion efforts at 0%.

  

## **Buying centers and stakeholder mapping**

  - Buying centers are defined by business units generating annual revenues exceeding 20 million dollars.
  - Frier operates through 7 distinct buying centers spanning pharma, medical devices, and other divisions.
  - Account mapping requires identifying specific stakeholders, decision makers, and influencers within each buying center.

  

## **Principles of professional growth and learning**

  - Sustainable career growth requires learning with perspective, practicing deliberately, and performing effectively.
  - Deliberate practice combines tracking efficiency metrics with measuring output quality to drive continuous improvement.

  

## **Relationship management engines**

  - Client engagement relies on activating both the conversion relationship engine and the build relationship engine.
  - The conversion engine transforms existing connections into revenue while the relationship engine establishes new connections.

  

## **Relationship Engines and Executive Targeting Strategy**

  - Mohamed outlined the dual strategy utilizing conversion and building engines for client engagement.
  - The strategy targets executives through 8 distinct outcomes including deals, board seats, and speaking opportunities.

  - Mohamed introduced a skill-tracking framework measuring metrics, exam scores, and practical performance for team members.
