AHMED FARHAN voiced skepticism regarding the tool's effectiveness, reporting that it currently sends fewer connection requests than expected — only 2 to 5 per day — and expressed doubt about its value. Given that only about 10 to 15 connections have been sent so far, Mohamed Anis instructed the team to subscribe and complete the initial set of messages to properly evaluate the platform's performance.
