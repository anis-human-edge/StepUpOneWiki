## **Unified Content Strategy**

  - Consolidated three separate blog topics—targeting startups, scaleups, and enterprise—into a single unified narrative to address the shared core challenge of securing invitations to high-value deals.
  - Identified that while the specific severity of the problem varies by company stage, the fundamental need for consistent departmental coverage and deal invitations remains a universal struggle.
  - Clarified that enterprise sales roles carry high stakes, where a lack of invitation to RFPs can result in personnel termination, mirroring the urgency felt by smaller ventures.

  

## **Core Sales Philosophy: The 'Decision Room' Concept**

  - Defined the central thesis 'Your deal is decided in rooms you never enter' to illustrate that sales success depends on internal champions advocating during private decision-making meetings.
  - Emphasized that success is not determined by the pitch alone but by the champion’s ability to defend the cause using pre-provided data, value propositions, and key talking points.
  - Instructed the team to avoid over-complicating straightforward concepts, urging for direct internalization rather than excessive, PhD-level analysis of simple truths.

  

## **Social Media Engagement and Professional Workflow**

  - Critiqued the team’s failure to engage with leadership’s social content, establishing a mandatory 1-hour window for reacting to posts.
  - Positioned social media engagement velocity as a critical indicator of professional responsiveness, noting that poor habits here mirror failures in client communication standards.
  - Mandated that future interactions must prioritize thoughtful, reflection-based commenting rather than passive, generic output.

  

## **Messaging Strategy and Content Refinement**

  - The team identified a lack of actionable solutions in current messaging regarding how to influence decisions made in rooms where the salesperson is not present.
  - Mohamed Anis updated the messaging sequence to emphasize relationship building as the primary driver for influencing deals in absentia.

  

## **Engagement Quality and AI Utilization**

  - Mohamed Anis critiqued current social media comments for being generic and stale, contrasting them with higher-quality human perspectives.
  - Mohamed Anis and Mahmud Bilow determined that suboptimal AI-generated comments stem from inadequate prompting rather than limitations of the tool itself.
  - The team established that maximizing AI output quality requires providing specific persona-based instructions, such as requesting a 20-year experience perspective.
