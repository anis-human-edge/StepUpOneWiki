### **Details**

  - **Pivot to Account Based Sales and Marketing**: Mohamed Anis introduced a strategic shift from general capital-based outreach to an Account Based Sales and Marketing model. Karen Todd questioned the distinction between this new approach and their current LinkedIn and email marketing efforts, noting that despite prior attempts, they have not seen significant engagement over the past year.
  - **Defining Account Based Sales and Marketing**: Mohamed Anis explained that the Account Based Sales and Marketing approach focuses on deep, account-by-account relationships rather than broad outreach. They distinguished between the "big things" that typically motivate salespeople—such as client dinners, demonstrations, and events—and the "small things" needed to nurture over a thousand stakeholders within a single account.
  - **Addressing White Spaces in B2B Accounts**: Mohamed Anis discussed the concept of "white spaces" in large organizations, noting that even with substantial revenue, firms often leave significant portions of accounts unpenetrated. They explained that this methodology targets B2B startups and scale-ups, focusing on building relationships with a small, specific set of key individuals, such as 10 to 100 people, rather than attempting massive, untargeted outreach.
  - **Three-Engine Sales System**: Mohamed Anis outlined a three-engine system to systematize sales efforts: a "connection engine" to maximize reaching 100% of contacts, an "engagement engine" to share valuable content without a sales pitch, and a "campaign engine" to eventually make a pitch. They explained that this system decouples these actions, with each engine running independently on a 30-day cycle to ensure relationships are properly warmed before any sales attempt.
  - **Lead Generation Challenges**: Karen Todd highlighted the tension between their current LinkedIn efforts and market competitors who use automated tools to secure direct clients. While Search Engine Optimization brings in leads for smaller retail jobs, they noted that larger, high-value deals require deep relational connections, which are currently lacking in the LinkedIn strategy.
  - **Applying Strategy to Slayton**: Mohamed Anis proposed applying the decoupling strategy to the Slayton project to drive meeting bookings. They suggested running parallel campaigns to reach inactive connections from the past. Karen Todd agreed, recommending they leverage existing data by downloading first-level LinkedIn connections to target specific potential clients.
  - **Question-Based Engagement**: Karen Todd proposed a shift toward "question-based" engagement rather than direct service promotion. They suggested asking prospects about specific challenges to prompt interaction. Mohamed Anis supported this, emphasizing that the engagement should warm up prospects without asking for anything in return.
  - **Simplifying Communication**: Mohamed Anis advised against packing messages with too much data or many questions. Instead, they recommended sending a short, highly readable blog post or a single, simple question to resonate with the prospect as a thought leader, without immediately asking for a meeting or sale.
  - **Content Creation Workflow**: Mohamed Anis offered to produce blog content based on transcripts of meetings to help Karen Todd establish thought leadership. They proposed recording future client meetings and using those transcripts to create short, effective blog posts, which can then be shared across the network to drive engagement.
  - **Integrating Stakeholders**: Karen Todd expressed a desire to include Zach in the strategy to leverage their family business background. Mohamed Anis suggested a "threesome" approach where Karen Todd acts as the thought leader while Zach is included in the connection loop, combining Karen Todd's larger reach with Zach's specific engagement opportunities.
  - **The Introduction and Referral Workflow**: Karen Todd described a highly effective workflow for generating meetings: after a successful conversation, they identify five to fifty key people in the contact's network. They then ask for introductions to the top five and reach out to the others by referencing the shared connection. This strategy ensures high-quality meetings with decision-makers like family office heads.
  - **Consultative Approach and Storytelling**: Karen Todd explained that their success stems from a non-sales, consultative approach, using storytelling to describe past client success and specific pain points rather than listing services. They emphasized that they position themself as a connector—advising, partnering, and offering tax strategies—which garners more respect and interest than direct pitching.
  - **Refining the Property Edge Strategy**: Mohamed Anis proposed pivoting the Property Edge website to categorize services into three phases: advisory, operation, and exit. They agreed to incorporate a blog section to capture Search Engine Optimization traffic and highlight Karen Todd's role as a thought leader, creating a more cohesive narrative for prospective clients.
  - **Leveraging Personal Networks for Capital Raising**: Mohamed Anis proposed a strategy to connect investors with projects by utilizing the existing professional networks of the team members. Mohamed Anis suggested that by acting as a "connector," they could facilitate three-way introductions between investors and project sponsors, thereby bypassing the need for investors to wait for or search for deal flow themselves.
  - **Curated Investment Matching**: Karen Todd highlighted the challenge of obtaining specific investor data, noting that while they have many credible developers, they need investors to provide 150 data points regarding their preferences to facilitate a curated experience. Karen Todd proposed using artificial intelligence to reverse-engineer these preferences from previous investments to provide highly personalized, matched deals.
  - **Service Tiers and Investor Engagement**: Karen Todd outlined a three-tiered structure for investor interaction: basic network visibility, highly filtered participation where investors provide data for analysis against potential sponsors, and a service level where sponsors pay for professional curation.
  - **Strategic Pivot for Property Edge**: Mohamed Anis announced a pivot for the Property Edge initiative, shifting the focus away from a model of raising capital, operating, and exiting to concentrate exclusively on capital raising through active curation. The refined strategy involves hand-delivering the top three investment opportunities to investors with an explanation of why they are the best fit, rather than relying on them to browse a website.
  - **Content Strategy and Thought Leadership**: Mohamed Anis and Karen Todd discussed the implementation of a thought leadership engine, with Mohamed Anis noting that high-quality blog content could drive the engagement campaign automatically. They agreed to experiment with distinct roles: Zach will manage the engagement engine by sharing pain points and experiences, while Karen Todd will oversee the thought leadership engine, focusing on capital conversations and stories.
