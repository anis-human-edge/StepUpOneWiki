## **Studio platform access and permissions**

  - Farah explained that platform access depends strictly on operational requirements.
  - Samatar noted discrepancies between individual account views and administrator interfaces.
  - Platform features restrict broad visibility to maintain security and focus.

  

## **Studio history and change tracking**

  - Farah demonstrated the history feature to track and undo recent workspace modifications.
  - The studio logs various user activities to facilitate error correction.

  

## **Client connection uploads and data structure**

  - Mohamed Anis stated that team leaders should handle initial uploads for the 5 clients.
  - Users must manage existing connections separately from new target connections.
  - Data exports from Sales Navigator or the CRM require proper column mapping to function correctly.

  

## **Relationship map zoom levels and deal outcomes**

  - Mohamed Anis explained that relationship graphs currently operate primarily at the company level.
  - Deals function as the smallest operational atom representing specific opportunities and outcomes.
  - The platform supports scaling relationship views from individual deals up to global company levels.

  

## **Account relationship mapping and buying centers**

  - Defining buying centers helps categorize incoming RFPs within client accounts.
  - Account relationship mapping connects specific representatives to departmental buying centers.
  - Outcome-based tracking serves as the primary metric for evaluating account management success.

  

## **Intelligence and wisdom modules**

  - Account intelligence compiles aggregated news and external data sources automatically.
  - Wisdom modules aggregate intelligence signals and job postings to generate predictive insights.
  - Automated data processing eliminates manual entry requirements for intelligence and wisdom features.

  

## **RFP timing and strategic value**

  - Engaging decision-makers before RFP publication carries significantly higher financial value.
  - Interacting with clients after RFP publication risks legal disqualification from the deal.
  - Proactive opportunity tracking enables early relationship building prior to formal proposal requests.

  

## **Platform workspaces and contact management**

  - System migration transitions clients to workspaces for platform exploration and contact management.
  - Manual contact tracking allows users to add key decision-makers involved in specific RFPs.
