### **Summary**

The meeting focused on structuring account-based marketing strategies and operationalizing sales engines via collaborative team frameworks.  
  
**Establishing Strategic Sales Engines**  
The framework outlines 3 distinct sales engines for account-based marketing: Connection, Nurturing, and Campaign. Maintaining these processes separately prevents operational confusion and ensures focus on specific engagement goals.  
  
**Defining Systematic Account Ownership**  
The team decided to implement a 3-level escalation hierarchy requiring 2 months of engagement per level to systematically penetrate target accounts. Assigning individual ownership prevents scattered, redundant outreach efforts.  
  
**Refining Nurturing and Collaboration**  
Nurturing requires providing value without immediate sales expectations, utilizing a 1-sentence, 1-link, 1-question messaging format. Collaboration is prioritized over individual efforts to maximize the effectiveness of account-based sales.
