# Metal.so

Fundraising automation platform. StepUp.One is in 30-day mastery mode (starting June 2026) to evaluate it as a client add-on and assess whether its features can be replicated internally.

## What It Does

Automates/semi-automates the fundraising process end-to-end for founders. Human operators (StepUp.One team) provide the active layer on top of the platform's AI. The combination is "human + Metal AI."

Exclusively designed for fundraising — cannot be used for other purposes.

## Traction

- ~10,000 founders from **Y Combinator** and **Textile** (Techstars) have adopted it
- Both accelerators have signed partnership agreements with Metal

- **Price to client:** ~$200–300/month additional
- **Choice:** StepUp.One's internal CRM or Metal CRM — client picks
