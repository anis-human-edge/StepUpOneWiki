- Leads daily analysis of team performance, identifying who is winning, why, and what messaging angles are working
- Identified a refined messaging strategy in June 2026 that improved response rates across the team

## Solopreneur Client Strategy

For clients who operate as solopreneurs and prefer not to scale:
- Position StepUp.One as a support mechanism, not a growth engine
- Message: "Stay small and remove hiding load — protect your time"
- Do not push for volume or rapid growth; respect the client's model
- Focus on the "behind the scenes work that still pulls on you"
