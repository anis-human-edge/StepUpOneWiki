## Role

- **Position:** Team leader / operations lead
- **Responsibilities:** Managing the outbound pipeline, coordinating team meeting follow-ups, handling team inquiries (15+ answered in a single morning), coaching team members on client strategy

## Key Contributions

- Runs the CRM pipeline, tracking all pending meetings and following up with team members to ensure contacts are converted
- Conducts client strategy sessions — notably designed the solopreneur strategy for clients who explicitly do not want to scale: "stay small but remove hiding load, protect your time"
- Leads daily analysis of team performance, identifying who is winning, why, and what messaging angles are working
- Identified a refined messaging strategy in June 2026 that improved response rates across the team
- Manages the meeting follow-up list (Derek, Kelvin, Lucas, Eton, Alliance, Sara, Jim J, Patrick) and ensures team members pursue confirmations

## Solopreneur Client Strategy

For clients who operate as solopreneurs and prefer not to scale:
- Position StepUp.One as a support mechanism, not a growth engine
- Message: "Stay small and remove hiding load — protect your time"
- Do not push for volume or rapid growth; respect the client's model
- Focus on the "behind the scenes work that still pulls on you"
