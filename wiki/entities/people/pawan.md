# Pawan

Warm inbound prospect. Had a 30-minute meeting with Mohamed Anis on Jun 24, 2026. Came via word of mouth. Anis gave him substantive product advice during the meeting. Likely to sign up but needs weeks — not days.

## Meeting Context

Pawan has a product that interested parties look at and then "run away." Anis was direct: he told Pawan his product wasn't ready for an effective GTM campaign. The advice was to fix a specific gap in the product before selling.

Pawan accepted this feedback and agreed to build "one new thing" that Anis asked him to build, then come back.

Anis to the team: "I gave him so much advice I basically told him he's not ready...so he said look you're right, I'm seeing that everybody wants to see it but once they see the product they run away. So let me build this one new thing and then I'll sell them."

## Status

Interested but not yet ready. Will sign up after the product improvement is done. Anis described both Lewis and Pawan meetings as "extremely positive."

## Lessons Noted

Anis flagged that he did not follow up with either Lewis or Pawan quickly enough: "We could have had another hundred more clients if I had followed up with all the clients I spoken to. Easily." He shared the website and signup links but did not prepare a detailed proposal.
