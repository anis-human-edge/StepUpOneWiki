## Oct 5, 2026 — LinkedIn Message Critique (CrowdStrike / Rosanna Martinez)

Nuuh presented a LinkedIn connection message she had sent to Rosanna Martinez (sales development manager at CrowdStrike, formerly a founding manager at Onum before its acquisition by CrowdStrike). The message, AI-generated and sent after approximately one minute of review, asked Rosanna about her outbound strategy "across two roles."

Mohamed Anis used this as the session's primary teaching case:
- The message was factually confused: Rosanna had not changed jobs — she held both roles simultaneously because CrowdStrike *acquired* Onum
- The message asked a peer-to-peer networking question inappropriate for a first contact
- Nuuh had not sized up Rosanna's background before sending (the interesting angle: founding manager at Onum → SDM at CrowdStrike after acquisition = big fish to small fish transition)

**Anis's verdict:** *"The most intelligent person I know spent one minute and sent a very stupid message."* The failure was not the AI output — it was the absence of critical judgment before executing it.

The session built an extended teaching framework around this case:
- 3-tier LinkedIn messaging system (lightweight Level 1 → intellectual contrast Level 3)
- Prospect research methodology (size them up before you message)
- AI output evaluation process

See: [LinkedIn Outreach Messaging](../../training-reskilling/linkedin-outreach-messaging.md)
