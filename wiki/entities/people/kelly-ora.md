# Kelly Ora

**Status:** Prospect (warm; meeting scheduled)
**Contact channel:** WhatsApp (directly with Mohamed Anis)

## Who She Is

Kelly Ora runs a **beta testing program for startup founders**. The program takes founders who have a product idea and want to validate it before reaching MVP stage — they go through her program, which tests their product and prepares them for the next stage of development and fundraising.

She had previously been in contact with Amina (StepUp.One team member) and was later re-qualified and re-engaged.

## Context (Aug 13, 2026)

Kelly reached out via Chol Akuien's LinkedIn indicating she was 8 hours behind the UK and proposing meeting times of 5:30pm or 6:30pm UK. Coordination initially went through Chol, but Mohamed Anis clarified he was communicating with her directly on WhatsApp and had already set up a call for **Friday, 2026-08-15 at 6:00pm UK**.

### What She Wanted

Kelly proposed a referral partnership: if StepUp.One sends startup founders to her beta testing program, she would (presumably) provide reciprocal leads or some form of compensation.

### StepUp.One's Response

**No referral.** Mohamed Anis explained the company's policy against referrals:
- StepUp.One cannot vouch for an external product it doesn't know well without trust capital already established
- Referring a client to an unknown third party is especially dangerous when StepUp.One itself is still building trust with those clients
- Referrals from a position of low trust compound the risk rather than generating value

**Instead, the offer:** manage Kelly Ora's LinkedIn profile to:
- Connect with 200 founders per month who match her criteria
- Educate those founders about her program
- Post, comment, and amplify her content to grow compound awareness
- Increase the number of people entering her program through sustained relationship building

This is StepUp.One acting as a **client** — running the GTM Engine on her behalf — rather than as a referral partner. StepUp.One is not involved as a brand; it works through her profile.

**Commercial model:** standard GTM service at $200/month (the tier for profile management and 200 connections/month). The proposal was to close this deal in the Aug 15 meeting.

  3. Frame the value: compound effect over 3–6 months, increasing program participants
