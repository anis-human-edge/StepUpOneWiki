# Lewis

Hot inbound prospect. Had a 30-minute meeting with Mohamed Anis on Jun 24, 2026. Mohamed Anis rated him "100% will sign up." Based in Australia (the meeting was at 9pm for Lewis). Came in purely through word of mouth — no one from the StepUp.One team is connected to him.

## How He Found StepUp.One

Lewis was at an event in San Francisco where someone (possibly Marco) told him about StepUp.One. He checked the team out and booked the meeting himself. This is a clean word-of-mouth acquisition — StepUp.One's compounding reputation effect at work.

## Meeting Arc

Lewis started the meeting skeptical — he thought StepUp.One might be "some b******* people." The arc of the 30 minutes:

- **Minute 0–10:** skeptical, barely interested
- **Minute 10–20:** "okay these guys are actually okay"
- **Minute 20–30:** "wow this is a real opportunity"
- **Minute 30:** "I have to work with these guys otherwise I will never survive"

By the end, Lewis was sharing his client names, friend's client names, and did not want to let the conversation end.

## Why This Meeting Matters

Mohamed Anis described the Lewis meeting as "a PhD thesis on pitching" — a master class in converting a skeptic. He specifically:
- Did not introduce himself with credentials or big-company references
- Switched the conversation from "what's your business" (deflected it) to learning about Lewis
- Found common ground quickly; used it to demonstrate understanding
- Relied on the core idea rather than personal authority

The team was told: "Watch the Lewis video in Beacon. You will be shocked. Whatever I say in 3 hours here, you will gain more in those 30 minutes."

## Status

Mohamed Anis is "100%" confident Lewis will sign up. No specific timeline given. Team has been told to follow up if they want.
