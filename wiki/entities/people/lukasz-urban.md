# Lukasz Urban

Fractional CFO and finance consultant building an AI-native cash flow management tool. Prospect and potential partner of StepUp.One. Contact: kontakt@cfosparring.com.

## Background

Lukasz operates as a one-man fractional CFO / finance implementation specialist. He had a strategic meeting with Mohamed Anis on 2026-06-16 (17:31 BST), followed by a Gemini export summary produced on 2026-07-25.

His firm (CFO Sparring) originally emerged from a focus on fractional CFO services. He is now building a dedicated cash flow management product with a live pilot using real data.

## The Cash Flow Tool

Lukasz is building an AI-native tool to answer questions like: *"Can I afford my marketing budget in August?"*

- **Primary function:** Cash flow analysis and liquidity management for companies with high transaction volumes
- **Target market (Lukasz's initial framing):** E-commerce and production businesses with $5–10M revenue struggling with liquidity, inventory management, delayed payments, insolvency risk
- **Anis's suggested refinement:** Expand to founder-led companies with $5–50M revenue lacking a full-time CFO

### Technical Architecture (as discussed with Anis)

- **Top-10 questions model:** Identify the 10 critical questions a founder/CEO needs answered across daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual dimensions. Deliver automatically through AI aggregation.
- **AI harness:** Flexible deployment system for financial data management, deployable quickly
- **Tiered pricing by model tier:**
  1. Frontier models (expensive, highest capability)
  2. Open-source models (mid-cost)
  3. On-premises deployment (client pays only for electricity)
- **Investor-facing angle:** Install on Stripe or similar to give investors portfolio-wide visibility — one command returns ARR, cash flow, or churn across all portfolio companies simultaneously

## Geographic Focus

Germany and Poland. Lukasz notes that Poland has evolved from a traditional "supply" (labor) market into a "demand" market, with growing economic confidence and labor costs converging toward Germany's.

## Relationship with StepUp.One

At the Jun 16 meeting, the agreed operating strategy was:
- Lukasz delegates all sales and marketing outreach to StepUp.One's team (~400 units/month)
- Lukasz focuses entirely on building the cash flow tool
- StepUp.One is interested in partnering on the AI-native system itself, not just running outreach for Lukasz

Anis's pitch: "Do not waste a minute of your time trying to do something that is not your cup of tea." The compounding model (20 daily connections, posts, comments, every day for 3 years) would build a pipeline Lukasz cannot manage on his own.

## StepUp.One's Philosophy Applied Here

Anis used this meeting to articulate the core delegation philosophy:

- **Understanding** is the one skill that cannot be outsourced to AI — the founder must maintain deep understanding of their domain
- **Judgment** (professional) and **taste** (creative) are the two skills to build in a team
- Everything else — LinkedIn, email outreach, Twitter, branding — can and should be delegated

Work quality control: proposals are reviewed by team member (Marva) → team leader → open forum before delivery.
