# Kushal

Current client (pausing service as of Jun 25, 2026). Running a GTM campaign managed by [Ahmed Farhan](../people/ahmed-farhan.md). Came via referral from Brendan Smith. Mohamed Anis was reluctant to take him on from day one.

## Status

**Pausing service** as of Jun 25, 2026. Kushal sent a message: "I would like to pause the service. To be honest, I haven't been seeing any meaningful responses so far." He is open to restarting "once I have figured out my messaging first before going on."

## Campaign Performance (week 4)

| Metric | Count | Rate |
|--------|-------|------|
| Contacted | 412 | — |
| Connected back | 47 | ~11% |
| Engaged | 5 | ~10% of connects |

Analysis: 11% connection rate is below the 20–30% target. 10% engagement of connects is acceptable. Root cause of client dissatisfaction: low connection rate, not enough "meaningful responses" in absolute terms.

## Compensation Model Dispute

Kushal proposed a pay-per-success model. StepUp.One refused — the platform operates exclusively on monthly subscriptions. The cost of the CRM, Sales Navigator, Crunchbase, and PhantomBuster makes pay-per-success financially unworkable. Ahmed Farhan was assigned to communicate this refusal clearly (Jun 25).

## Campaign Notes

- Target list is difficult: "people who are very busy" — low response rates are expected
- First month is relationship building; meetings are not expected until month 3-4
- Messaging sequence: first message sent, second message sent; 5 people engaged, none negatively
- The team acknowledges they may have failed to set expectations clearly enough at the sales stage
- Anis originally did not want this client; accepted only due to Brendan Smith's referral

## Lessons Learned

Mohamed Anis used this account as a teaching case on Jun 25:
- "We can learn from people like him rather than earn"
- "When I look at these observations I don't know whether they are meaningful engagement or not meaningful" — need better dashboard design
- A full sales assembly pipeline (showing all response scenarios and how to handle them) would help clients understand the process
- The Edocite-style dashboard could be adapted for Kushal to show campaign performance visually

## Open Items

- Ahmed Farhan to respond to Kushal: refuse pay-per-success, clarify monthly subscription basis
- Team to rebuild the dashboard if it cannot answer client questions about campaign performance
- Sales assembly pipeline to be built (Ahmed Farhan) — directly triggered by this client situation
