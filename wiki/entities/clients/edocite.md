# Edocite

Active client. An EdTech company that helps enrollment teams engage every inquiry instantly without burning out staff. StepUp.One runs their outbound GTM campaign and built their performance dashboard.

## What They Do

Edocite's product is for enrollment teams — educational institutions managing large volumes of inbound student inquiries. Their value proposition: engage every inquiry instantly, without burning out staff.

Outbound message used for Edocite: *"I saw your work in [institution]. I work with enrollment teams on how to engage every inquiry instantly without burning out staff."*

## StepUp.One Engagement

- StepUp.One is running outbound on their behalf, reaching out to decision-makers at educational institutions
- A campaign dashboard has been built for Edocite to track outreach performance
- Mohamed Anis referenced the Edocite dashboard on Jun 25 as the model for what all clients should receive: "Can the dashboard that we have built for Edocite answer all his questions? If not then we have to go and rebuild it."
