### 7-Step Onboarding Process

1. **Service Activation** — Sign up via Stripe at stepup.one/founder-gtm; click "Pay & Join"
2. **LinkedIn Access Setup** — Share LinkedIn login credentials for campaign execution
3. **LinkedIn Profile Optimization** — Team rewrites positioning, messaging, and profile structure if not already optimized
4. **Communication Channel Setup** — WhatsApp group with CEO, Team Leader, and Campaign Specialist
5. **CRM Implementation** — Dedicated CRM for tracking all campaign activities and pipeline
6. **ICP Sourcing & Messaging Development** — Identify Ideal Customer Profile; develop tailored messaging sequence
7. **Campaign Review & Launch** — Walk-through of targeting, messaging, and workflow; approval; launch
