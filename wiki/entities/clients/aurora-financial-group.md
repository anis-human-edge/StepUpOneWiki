### Curated Investor Matchmaking Platform

Aurora identified a gap in the market for a "white glove" matchmaking-style service for property investors. Existing platforms are noisy and filled with service providers rather than quality investor connections.

**Strategic decisions made (2026-06-29):**
- Focus exclusively on approximately 12 best-in-class sponsors within resilient asset classes
- Priority asset classes: **medical** and **veterinary** (essential, economically resilient)
- Move beyond simple data storage — build an engagement strategy that matches sponsors with investors based on specific thesis criteria
- Avoid mass-market approach; curated intelligence over volume (analogous to modern dating apps vs. older matching models)

**Competitive context:** Anis demonstrated a third-party startup-focused platform (~$600) that filters investors by "thesis" — a feature not standard in existing property databases. The team debated building similar functionality for the property investor context.
