# Ferrarx

Food-as-medicine delivery company. StepUp.One client — the team runs outbound prospecting to win new insurance company clients for Ferrarx.

## Business Model

Ferrarx cooks and delivers medically tailored food to chronic disease patients (diabetes, heart disease, kidney disease, etc.) on behalf of insurance companies. The pitch to insurers: it is far cheaper to send a patient the right food every day ($5,000/year) than to pay for a catastrophic medical intervention later ($1 million+). Food is medicine — prevent the crisis before it happens.

**Who pays:** Insurance companies — not hospitals, not healthcare providers, not individual patients. The food is a benefit delivered to the insurer's high-risk or elderly enrollees.

**Who receives the food:** Chronic disease patients insured by the client's customers — elderly populations, high-risk individuals (diabetes, kidney disease, heart conditions).

**Key distinction Ahmed Farhan learned on 26 Jun 2026:** Hospitals and healthcare providers are NOT the buyer. They treat patients but do not insure them and do not send food. Only insurance companies that have insured a population and want to reduce long-term claims costs are the right target.

## Current Client (of Ferrarx)

- **Independence Blue Cross (IBX)** — Ferrarx's only existing long-term client (~4–5 years). The only Blue Cross company Ferrarx currently serves.
- IBX is a specific Blue Cross entity, not "Blue Cross" in general. There are ~193 distinct companies with "Blue Cross" in their name and ~128 "Blue Shield" companies — each is a separate organization.
