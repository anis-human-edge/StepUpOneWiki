# Dodo

Active ABSM client. StepUp.One team runs LinkedIn commenting and relationship-building campaigns on Dodo's behalf, targeting enterprise accounts across the UK, Germany, California, and New York.

## Engagement Model

StepUp.One serves as Dodo's ABSM execution team:
- **LinkedIn commenting engine** deployed via the [Commenting Studio](../systems/commenting-studio.md)
- **10–20 comments/day** targeting CXO, VP, and director-level posts
- **Richard approval workflow**: all draft responses reviewed and approved by Richard for the first 2 months
- **Connection requests combined** with commenting strategy for maximum impact
- **Voice profile** being built in the Commenting Studio for authentic tone matching

## Target Geography & Accounts

Relationship health metrics (as of Oct 2026):
- Standard Chartered: 50%
- HSBC: 70%
- DNB: 20%

Geographic focus: 20 VPs across UK, Germany, California, New York.

## Team Assignment

- **Farah Ibrahim** — primary execution lead; manages commenting engine, Richard relationship, identifies low-hanging leads
- **The full team** — evaluates the commenting engine's effectiveness on behalf of Dodo and reports findings

## Client Contact

**Richard** — internal approver/decision-maker on the Dodo account. Reviews and approves all draft LinkedIn comments for the first 2 months. Voice profile for Richard is being created in the Commenting Studio.

## Strategy Notes

High-level professionals prefer direct private communication over public comments. The strategy combines commenting with connection requests for a more equal, peer-level approach. Mohamed Anis advises against public congratulatory posts for unknown individuals — private messages preferred.
