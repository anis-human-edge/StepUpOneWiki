# Size Mine

AI-driven sizing solutions company. StepUp.One is engaged to help them raise capital, execute a GTM strategy, and assist with international expansion.

## What They Do

Size Mine provides AI-powered sizing technology targeting the **$30 billion clothing return market** — the problem of wrong-size purchases that generate massive returns costs across the apparel industry.

## StepUp.One Engagement

**Services delivered (3 pillars):**
1. Raising capital
2. Executing GTM strategies
3. Assisting Korean firms with international expansion

**Target markets:** US and European markets (not the Korean domestic market — international expansion is the priority).

**Communication context:** The team is navigating language/communication barriers with the client. AI tools are being used to bridge language gaps.
